Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take to work?

Most sites see early movement within a few months, but meaningful results often take six months or more depending on competition, site health and content. No one can honestly guarantee rankings or timelines.

What does a technical SEO audit include?

Crawling and indexing checks, site structure, page speed, mobile usability, duplicate content, metadata, internal links and structured data, followed by a prioritized fix list.

How does your link building work?

I research relevant sites, then do manual outreach and content-led link earning. I avoid spammy networks and purchased link schemes that can put your site at risk.

Do you offer local SEO in Lahore?

Yes. This covers Google Business Profile optimization, local citations, location-focused content and review strategy for businesses serving Lahore and nearby areas.

Can you help with international SEO?

Yes. I work with international clients remotely, including market research, language and location targeting and technical setup.

Do you build WordPress websites?

Yes. I build business sites, landing pages and redesigns with SEO-friendly structure and good performance.
